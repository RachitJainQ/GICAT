Where Institutional Capital Goes During an "Everything Sell-Off"

1. The Core Premise: Global De-Risking vs. Sector Rotation

When institutional investors simultaneously liquidate US equities (SPX), Indian equities (NIFTY 50), Bitcoin (BTC/USD), Gold (XAU/USD), and Long-Duration US Treasury Bonds (TLT), the market is undergoing a systemic liquidity contraction or carry-trade deleveraging. Capital does not disappear; it moves into three distinct buckets tracked across this terminal:

Bucket 1: Live Charted Destinations (90% of Flows)

Capital parks in overnight repos & 0-3M US T-Bills (SGOV), drives up the US Dollar (DXY), or repays cheap funding currencies like the Japanese Yen (USD/JPY dropping). Meanwhile, geographic value rotators buy deep-value indices (HSI/SXXP/UKX), physical supply-shock commodities (BCOM/DBA), or long-volatility tail hedges (VIX).

Bucket 2: Off-Exchange Benchmark Indices

Trillions of dollars sit in bilateral OTC deals and private markets tracked via listed institutional bellwethers and daily benchmarks: Private Credit (BXSL), Private Equity (BX), Commercial Real Estate (PLD), Private Infrastructure (BIP), Managed Futures (DBMF), High-Yield Credit Spreads (BAMLH0A2HYB), and Maritime Shipping (BDI).

Bucket 3: The Remaining 10% Rare Condition Tail

Uncorrelated or contingent assets where transactions spike only under rare triggers: Distressed Debt/Special Situations (BAM), LP PE Secondaries (STEP), Catastrophe Reinsurance (RNR), Litigation Finance (BUR), Carbon Quotas (KRBN), Physical Uranium (SRUUF), and Water Rights (NQH2O).
